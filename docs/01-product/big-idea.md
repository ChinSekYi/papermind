# Papermind Big Idea

## What Papermind is
- Decision support system for ML engineers using research papers.
- Turns paper reading into practical engineering decisions over time.

## Problem
- ML engineers read many papers but cannot systematically compare and reuse what they learned.
- This causes repeated reading, slower decisions, and wasted time.

User voice:
- "I read many papers, but I cannot efficiently compare them or reuse what I learned when building systems."

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- Role: Applied ML engineer or tech lead.
- Company: Seed to Series B startups shipping AI products quickly.
- Job: Compare new papers weekly and decide what to implement.
- Pain: Reading 20 papers is too time-consuming and key methods are missed.
- Trigger: Model quality plateaus, feature deadlines, competitive pressure.

Good fit users:
- Read papers frequently.
- Suffer from information overload.
- Have real delivery stakes (not casual reading).

## Wedge Ideas + Differentiation

### 1) Method comparison for shipping decisions
What Papermind delivers:
- "Compare top 5 methods for my use case" with trade-offs on data, compute, implementation complexity, and expected gains.

Why this is different:
- Not just summary output like "this paper proposes X".
- Gives decision-oriented output like "use method A if dataset < 1M and latency < 200ms".

ROI metric:
- Time-to-decision drops from days to hours.

### 2) Evidence packs for product and executive decisions
What Papermind delivers:
- A concise, citation-backed brief for one business question (for example, "Should we adopt RAG reranking method X?").

Why this is different:
- Focuses on trustworthy, decision-ready output instead of generic chat.

ROI metric:
- Fewer bad bets and faster team alignment.

### 3) Domain-specialized paper intelligence
What Papermind delivers:
- Better extraction and comparison in one niche (for example, LLM evaluation, agentic workflows, or medical AI).

Why this is different:
- General tools are broad but shallow; Papermind goes deep in one domain.

ROI metric:
- Higher precision and fewer hallucinated or misclassified insights.

### 4) Team workflow and research memory
What Papermind delivers:
- Weekly "what changed" briefs.
- Shared research memory and team knowledge base.
- Team awareness signals (for example, "new papers uploaded by tech lead that you have not reviewed").

Why this is different:
- Built for team learning and reuse, not one-off solo reading.

ROI metric:
- Higher knowledge reuse and less duplicate research work.

## MVP (simple)
User flow:
1. Upload 3 to 5 papers.
2. Extract structured fields:
   - Problem
   - Method
   - Dataset
   - Metrics
3. Generate automatic comparison output.

## Execution Plan (now)
- Talk to 5 target users.
- Build MVP in 3 to 7 days.
- Show concrete demo outputs.

Reason:
- Users react better to something real than to abstract ideas.
